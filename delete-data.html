Duckdoku Data Deletion

Duckdoku has no accounts and stores no personal data on our servers. Your progress is saved only on your phone.

  1. To delete your data: open Android Settings → Apps → Duckdoku → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  2. To reset only game progress: open the game, tap the gear icon, then Reset progress.
  3. Ad and purchase data is held by Google. See Google's privacy policy.
